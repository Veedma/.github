# Veedma

**Testosterone care that helps your body produce more of its own.**

Veedma is a preventive men's health telehealth clinic focused on hormone, sexual, and fertility health. We treat low testosterone (secondary and functional hypogonadism), male fertility concerns, and erectile dysfunction, and the symptoms men actually feel: libido, erection, energy, strength, and mood, in men 18 and up. Care is fully online and available in most US states (not currently Alabama, Alaska, District of Columbia, Hawaii, Mississippi, New Jersey, New York, Oklahoma, Rhode Island, Utah, or Wisconsin).

About 86% of low testosterone is secondary or functional rather than primary testicular failure (European Male Aging Study, NEJM 2010), so we diagnose the cause before reaching for replacement. Our clinical approach is natural-stimulation-first: Enclomiphene, a SERM that signals the pituitary to release more LH and FSH and raises the body's own testosterone production while supporting spermatogenesis, is the foundation. Men who also have erection difficulties or urinary symptoms get an Enclomiphene + Tadalafil 5 mg combination tablet, a single daily oral medication compounded by our FDA-registered partner pharmacy. TRT is reserved for the minority with primary hypogonadism. Protocols follow AUA, AACE, and Endocrine Society guidelines, and treatment targets symptoms and Free Testosterone in range rather than Total T over 1000.

Diagnostics run on an in-person Men's Health panel through CLIA-certified partner labs: Total and Free Testosterone by Equilibrium Dialysis with LC-MS/MS, plus LH, FSH, Estradiol, CBC, CMP, Vitamin D, PSA (age 40+), and Insulin (BMI > 25). Every marker changes a treatment decision or flags a safety risk. Recent outside results from the past 180 days can be uploaded instead, and anyone can upload existing labs for a free clinical review.

The direct-pay subscription is all-inclusive: daily medication, all lab work, video visits with licensed providers, dose adjustments, unlimited messaging with the care team, and free shipping. Plans run $179/month with no commitment down to $99/month on an annual plan; the initial diagnostic panel is $99 for new patients. Follow-up labs come after the first month of treatment and every 6 months after. No insurance, no membership-plus-medication fine print, no surprise lab bills. FSA/HSA eligible.

Veedma is LegitScript-certified. Health information is handled as protected health information under HIPAA and the California CMIA, and is never sold.

Veedma is founder-led. Vladimir Kotlov, MD (CEO) is a urologist by training (MD, 2014) with clinical experience in men's reproductive health, and the clinical voice in every product decision. Victor Kuznetsov (CTO) was previously Co-Founder and CTO of Yva.ai (people analytics, acquired by Visier) and owns the Veedma platform end-to-end.

The clinical platform is AI-native by default: production code calls OpenAI for in-product features and the content pipeline, the team works in Claude Code day-to-day. Akute is the EHR, Stripe handles billing.

Veedma Inc. is a Delaware C-Corporation, headquartered in San Francisco, with a remote team across the US. Veedma Inc. provides technology and administrative support; medical care is delivered by AYA PCs, an independent professional entity, and its state-licensed clinicians.
