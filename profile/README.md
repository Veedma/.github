# Veedma

**Telehealth for men's hormonal and sexual health. Restore natural testosterone before resorting to TRT.**

Veedma is a telehealth practice for men's hormonal and sexual health. We treat low testosterone (hypogonadism), erectile dysfunction, and the symptoms men actually feel: libido, erection, energy, strength, and mood, in men 18 and up. Care is fully online and available across most US states.

Most low testosterone in men is secondary and reversible, not primary testicular failure, so we diagnose the cause before reaching for replacement. Our clinical approach is natural-stimulation-first: Enclomiphene, a SERM that raises the body's own testosterone production while preserving fertility, is the foundation. When patients present with low Free Testosterone alongside erectile dysfunction or lower-urinary-tract symptoms, Tadalafil is co-prescribed in the same monthly delivery. Vitamin D3 when labs indicate. Testosterone replacement therapy is not a service we currently offer. Protocols follow AUA and EAU guidelines.

Our cash-pay subscription is all-inclusive: video consultations with licensed physicians, comprehensive lab panels twice yearly (Total and Free Testosterone by LC-MS/MS, the full hormonal axis, safety markers), compounded medications shipped, unlimited messaging with the medical team, and dose adjustments. No insurance, no surprise fees. FSA/HSA eligible. The initial diagnostic fee is refunded if we can't approve treatment.

Veedma is founder-led. Vladimir Kotlov, MD (CEO) is a urologist by training (MD, 2014) with clinical experience in men's reproductive health, and the clinical voice in every product decision. Victor Kuznetsov (CTO) was previously Co-Founder and CTO of Yva.ai (people analytics, acquired by Visier) and owns the Veedma platform end-to-end.

The clinical platform is AI-native by default: production code calls OpenAI for in-product features and the content pipeline, the team works in Claude Code day-to-day. Akute is the EHR, Stripe handles billing.

Veedma Inc. is a Delaware C-Corporation, headquartered in San Francisco, with a remote team across the US.
